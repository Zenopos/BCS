Service Categories

What BCS can contract for your facility.

Janitorial & Custodial Services

Recurring custodial programs, day porter support, commercial cleaning, sanitization, restroom support, and floor care for offices, public buildings, schools, and commercial facilities.

Use cases: daily custodial contracts, office cleaning programs, facility sanitization, common-area upkeep.

Landscaping & Grounds

Grounds maintenance, mowing and trimming programs, bed maintenance, exterior cleanup, and seasonal site upkeep coordinated for municipal, campus, and commercial properties.

Use cases: recurring grounds contracts, municipal site upkeep, commercial campus maintenance.

Pressure Washing & Surface Restoration

Building exteriors, sidewalks, parking structures, loading areas, and site surfaces — scheduled programs or project-based work with appropriate containment and reporting.

Use cases: building facade cleaning, garage and walkway programs, pre-inspection exterior preparation.

Hauling & Waste Removal

Debris removal, bulk item hauling, and waste-removal support for facility turnovers, site projects, and recurring disposal needs.

Use cases: unit turnovers, site cleanouts, storm debris response, recurring bulk removal.

Commercial / Interior Painting

Interior and exterior painting scopes for commercial and public-sector facilities — coordinated alongside related trades where projects overlap.

Use cases: common-area repaints, turnover painting, exterior refresh scopes.

Recurring Facility Support

Multi-trade recurring service agreements combining any of the categories above under one contract, one schedule, and one point of accountability.

Use cases: bundled facility services, multi-site programs, annual service agreements.

How Delivery Works

BCS provides the contract and coordination layer.

BCS does not operate as a staffing agency or a dispatch platform. Each requirement is scoped, contracted, and assigned to qualified service partners in the BCS network. BCS remains responsible to the client for communication, scheduling, quality, and closeout documentation.

Pricing is quoted per requirement. BCS does not publish rate cards; government buyers receive pricing through eVA Quick Quote or solicitation response, and commercial clients receive a scoped proposal.

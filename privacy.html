Privacy Policy.

How Benchmark Contract Services LLC handles information submitted through this website.

Last updated: September 2026

Information We Collect

When you submit a form on this site, we collect the information you provide: name, organization, contact details, client category, services requested, facility or service information, any optional uploaded SOW/RFQ/solicitation document, and message content. We do not collect sensitive identifiers (tax IDs, insurance certificates) through website forms.

How We Use It

Submitted information is used solely to respond to your inquiry, scope a service requirement, or evaluate a service partner application. We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties for marketing.

Form Processing & Security

This site uses Netlify Forms. Submissions are transmitted over HTTPS and stored securely; optional file attachments are base64-encoded in transit and handled by Netlify's forms infrastructure. This site is served exclusively over HTTPS with HSTS and restrictive security headers. No tracking pixels or third-party advertising scripts are used.

Data Retention

Inquiry records are retained only as long as needed to conduct business and meet any applicable public-procurement record requirements.

Your Rights

To request correction or deletion of information you have submitted, contact info@benchmarkcontractservices.com.

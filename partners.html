How the Bench Works

Contract flow for service partners.

  1. 01

    Tier 1 intake — zero friction

    Tell us your trade, capacity, service area, and standard rate structure. No COI or W-9 required at this stage.

  2. 02

    Bench placement

    Approved partners are slotted by trade and territory, with first right of refusal on matching scopes.

  3. 03

    Scoped assignments

    When a contract lands, BCS issues a defined scope, schedule, and rate. You execute; BCS handles the client.

  4. 04

    Net-7 payment

    Verified completed work is paid on net-7 terms — not net-30, not when the client pays.

Who should apply

Established operators in janitorial/custodial, commercial cleaning, landscaping and grounds, pressure washing, hauling and waste removal, or painting — with their own equipment, crews, and a track record of completing contracted work.

This is a subcontractor network, not employment, not a franchise, and not a lead marketplace. BCS holds the client contract; partners hold the work.

What partners get

Contract opportunities without sales overhead. BCS manages scope disputes, client communication, documentation, and collections. Partners focus on execution.
